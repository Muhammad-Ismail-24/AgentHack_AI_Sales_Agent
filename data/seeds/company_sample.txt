Swiftlink Automation

Swiftlink Automation is a B2B SaaS company based in Dubai, UAE, founded in
2019. We build workflow automation software for logistics and freight
forwarding companies across the Middle East, helping them replace manual,
spreadsheet-driven operations with automated, real-time systems.

Our Services:

1. WhatsApp Automation Suite - Automates customer notifications, delivery
   status updates, proof-of-delivery collection, and support ticket triage
   directly over WhatsApp Business API. Built specifically for logistics
   companies that rely on WhatsApp as their primary customer channel in the
   GCC market. Typical customers see a 40% drop in "where is my order" phone
   calls within the first month.

2. Warehouse Management System (WMS) - Real-time inventory tracking, pick-
   and-pack workflow optimisation, and barcode/RFID scanning integration.
   Designed for warehouses handling 500 to 50,000 SKUs.

3. Fleet & Dispatch Optimisation - Route planning, driver assignment, and
   live GPS tracking for last-mile delivery fleets. Includes a driver mobile
   app and a dispatcher web dashboard.

4. Customs & Compliance Automation - Automated document generation and
   validation for cross-border shipments within the GCC, reducing customs
   clearance delays.

Who We Serve:
