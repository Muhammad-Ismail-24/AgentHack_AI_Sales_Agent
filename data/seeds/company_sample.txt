NovaTech Solutions — Company Profile

ABOUT US
NovaTech Solutions is an AI software company founded in 2022, headquartered in
Karachi, Pakistan, with a regional office in Dubai, UAE. We are a team of 25 —
engineers, ML researchers, and solution architects — and we build conversational
AI and workflow automation for mid-market companies that have outgrown manual
customer operations.

We work almost entirely with businesses that live on WhatsApp: logistics
operators fielding shipment queries, e-commerce brands handling order status
requests, and real-estate agencies juggling viewing enquiries. These companies
typically have 5 to 40 people answering the same questions over and over.

WHAT WE SELL

1. WhatsApp AI Chatbot — from $2,500/month
   A conversational agent on WhatsApp Business API that handles customer support
   end to end: order tracking, shipment status, FAQs, appointment booking, and
   escalation to a human when confidence drops. Understands English, Arabic, and
   Urdu. Typical deployment takes 3 to 4 weeks. Customers usually see 60 to 70
   percent of inbound messages fully resolved without a human.

2. CRM Integration — from $1,800/month
   Connects WhatsApp, email, and web chat into Salesforce, HubSpot, or Zoho, so
   every conversation lands against the right customer record. Includes
   bidirectional sync, custom field mapping, and automated lead routing. Removes
   the copy-paste work that eats several hours per rep per week.

3. AI Email Assistant for sales teams — from $1,200/month
   Drafts personalised outreach and replies from your own CRM data and product
   docs, classifies inbound replies by intent, and queues follow-ups. Sales teams
   report roughly 3x more first-touch volume per rep without extra headcount.

4. Custom LLM Solutions — project-based, from $25,000
   Retrieval-augmented systems built on a company's own documents: internal
   knowledge assistants, document processing pipelines, and domain-specific
   copilots. Includes evaluation harnesses and on-premise deployment where data
   residency requires it.

CASE STUDIES

Gulf Freight Services (Dubai, UAE — 180 employees, logistics)
Their 12-person support team was handling around 3,000 WhatsApp messages a week,
mostly "where is my shipment". Average first response time was 4 hours and got
worse over weekends. We deployed the WhatsApp AI Chatbot integrated with their
TMS. Within six weeks, 68 percent of messages were resolved with no human
involved, first response time fell to under 2 minutes, and they redeployed 5 of
the 12 support staff onto account management. Support cost per ticket dropped 61
percent.

Zahra Retail Group (Karachi, Pakistan — 90 employees, e-commerce)
Order-status enquiries spiked every campaign launch and their team could not keep
up, so carts were abandoned after purchase. We combined the WhatsApp AI Chatbot
with CRM Integration into their Shopify and HubSpot stack. Repeat purchase rate
rose 23 percent over two quarters, and the support team handled a 3x volume
increase during Ramadan campaigns with no new hires.

WHO WE SELL TO
Companies with 50 to 500 employees in logistics, e-commerce, and real estate,
concentrated in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Pakistan. Our sweet spot is a business
with a high volume of repetitive inbound customer messages, an existing CRM, and
a support team that is visibly underwater.

TECHNOLOGY
Claude (Anthropic) for reasoning and generation, LangGraph for agent
orchestration, Qdrant for vector retrieval, WhatsApp Business API via Twilio,

optional on-premise for data-residency requirements.
